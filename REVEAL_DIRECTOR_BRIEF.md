# LC App Reveal Director Polish

Status: active presentation-only polish branch.
Baseline: current main after Visual 2.0 merge.
Target: 9.5/10 self-guided public reveal.

## Core thesis
Live Casino has personalities. Traditional casino discovery hides them behind Game → Table → Seat. LC App changes the discovery model to Person/Creator → Content → Follow → Live → licensed operator handoff → Return.

Memorable line: **Casino was built around games. LC is built around people.**

LC App owns discovery, creator identity, social/content, schedules/reminders, context/attribution and handoff. Licensed operators/providers retain game operation, wallet, deposits/withdrawals, KYC/AML, wagering and settlement.

## Director diagnosis
The current reveal is visually credible and functionally deep, but it still behaves too much like a product demo. The next quality gain must come from narrative compression and payoff, not more features.

### P0 presentation improvements
1. Opening must explain the shift in under 10 seconds. Replace internal/version language such as `Visual 2.0` with audience-facing language. The first viewport should communicate: old model = Game → Table → Seat; LC model = Discover → Creator → Follow → Live → Play/Handoff → Return.
2. Remove SIGN IN / CREATE ACCOUNT from the public reveal entry. Registration is not part of the reveal and distracts from the concept.
3. Make Player the canonical guided journey. Primary CTA should start the story; Operator/Provider should remain optional ecosystem perspectives rather than competing entry choices.
4. Strengthen creator transformation. The audience must see `Dealer at a table` become `followable creator/persona` with profile, content, schedule and audience.
5. Strengthen Operator/Provider payoff. Do not present generic dashboards. Explain the business mechanism visually: Creator → Audience → Live intent → operator-controlled handoff → return loop. Provider value = distribution/discovery through people without LC taking over regulated game operation.
6. End with a clear thesis/payoff screen rather than simply another app state. The final impression should answer: what changed, who benefits, and what LC does not replace.

### P1 polish
- Prefer short human copy over compliance-heavy explanatory text in hero surfaces. Keep factual/legal boundary available but secondary.
- Remove visible internal labels such as `Visual 2.0`, `Demo Reviewer`, `Demo Company`, `Industry reviewer` from hero/public presentation surfaces where they are not needed for factuality.
- Keep illustrative entities clearly non-real without repeatedly shouting `Demo` in every visual beat.
- Reduce repeated cards after the core point is understood. One strong cinematic state beats three redundant UI cards.
- Maintain dark graphite + teal/mint visual system, strong photography, large typography and restrained motion.
- Mobile 390 and desktop 1440 are release gates.

## Required reveal sequence
1. **Problem** — Live Casino personalities are hidden behind lobby/game/table navigation.
2. **Shift** — discover the person first.
3. **Player** — Discover → Creator → Follow → Live → operator handoff → Return.
4. **Creator** — identity → content → schedule → audience → live presence.
5. **Operator** — creator-led intent arrives with context; operator remains in control of regulated play.
6. **Provider** — games/tables gain an additional people-led discovery layer.
7. **Boundary** — LC does not become wallet, casino operator, KYC/AML or wagering layer.
8. **Payoff** — `Live Casino through people.` / `Casino was built around games. LC is built around people.`

## Acceptance gates
- A first-time iGaming executive can explain LC App correctly after 60–90 seconds without founder narration.
- No invented customers, integrations, pilots, revenue, licenses, approvals or production-readiness claims.
- No public reveal dependency on authentication.
- Golden path remains interactive.
- No console errors.
- Responsive at 390 and 1440.
- Presentation purpose remains separate from future standalone application/productization.
- Do not merge cosmetic changes that do not materially improve comprehension or emotional impact.
